01 / The problem

Registration shouldn't take three browser tabs and a spreadsheet.

Every quarter, UW Bothell students juggle MyPlan, the Time Schedule, and their degree audit at the same time — manually cross-referencing which sections are open, which ones collide, and which prerequisites they've actually cleared. It's tedious, easy to get wrong, and it punishes the students with the most constraints: jobs, commutes, and care responsibilities.

SmartScheduler collapses that workflow into a single sentence. It reads the live UW Bothell Time Schedule, understands a student's real-world constraints, and does the cross-referencing for them — so planning a quarter feels like asking an advisor, not auditing a database.

  • ✕ Tabs that don't talk to each other
  • ✕ Hidden time conflicts between sections
  • ✕ Prerequisites buried in the catalog
  • ✕ No quick "what fits my week?" answer
02 / What it does

Section-level scheduling that respects your real constraints.

Natural-language interface

Type your needs the way you'd say them out loud. No filters, no forms — just a sentence.

Section-level awareness

Recommendations name the exact section and meeting times — not just a course code.

Day & time constraints

Block out Fridays, mornings, or a work shift. The planner only offers sections that fit.

Credit-load limits

Set a ceiling and the schedule stays under it — ideal for working students balancing a part-time load.

Prerequisite enforcement

Courses you're not eligible for are filtered out before they're ever suggested.

Conflict detection

Every returned schedule is checked for overlapping meeting times — guaranteed clash-free.

03 / Under the hood

An AI that proposes — and code that disposes.

SmartScheduler pairs a retrieval-augmented language model with a deterministic validator. The model is creative; the validator is strict. You only ever see a schedule that survives both.

05 / Using it

Three steps to a planned quarter.

  1. 01

    Describe your quarter

    Open the app and type what you need in plain English — e.g. "finish my CSS core requirements, only on campus Tuesday and Thursday, under 14 credits." Optionally list the courses you've already completed.

  2. 02

    Review your recommendations

    SmartScheduler returns specific sections with their meeting times and a short rationale, drawn from the live UW Bothell Time Schedule and filtered to courses you're eligible for.

  3. 03

    Confirm it's conflict-free

    Each schedule is pre-checked for time clashes and unmet prerequisites. See a green all-clear, then take it to MyPlan to register with confidence.
